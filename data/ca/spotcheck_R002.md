## Boundaries of the check

The Housing for All disclaimer attributes opinions/recommendations to authors and says these need not reflect publishers or funders. Group authorship supports E00203; it must not independently establish endorsement by every employer merely listed after an individual's name. The roster mixes named organizations and individuals with affiliations. Distinguishing those was not re-audited across all47 derived rows in this13-row sample.

No false quotation, unsupported supporter attribution in sampled rows, or incorrect BC adoption date was found. Two recurrence classifications require conservative review (E00125, E00126; likely same E00127). Parent was notified promptly; this report does not mutate CSVs.
