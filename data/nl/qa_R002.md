## Wealth-tax definition cross-check

The bill is an actual annual stock tax, rather than merely a Box 3 tax on assumed or realised income. [36173-2](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36173-2.html), articles 3.1–3.3, sets a calendar-year wealth base, deducts debts and excludes assets generating Box 1/Box 2 income. Article 3.10 gives a EUR 100,000 allowance; article 4.1 gives progressive 1–5% bands. `partial_wealth` is accurate for E00043–E00051. Bill support must not be represented as enacted law.

## Recommended correction

For ORG_E006 (FNV Noord), prefer `annual_net_wealth` to `billionaire_minimum`. The publication endorses a direct 2% tax on Quote-500 fortunes and explicitly discusses the list's EUR 140m entry threshold. It does not specify a minimum/top-up mechanism, and the advocated population is not limited to billionaires. Keep the 2% rate and Quote-500 scope in `design_detail`, with no invented tax allowance. No quotation/date/grade changes are required for the sample.
