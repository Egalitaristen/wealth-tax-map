## Individual Dutch roll call: scope matters

The [23 April 2024 original parliamentary record](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/h-tk-20232024-68-33.html) contains 72 individually named affirmative votes on motion 25087-331, with a stated result of 72/71. This is an actual individual roll call, unlike the party hand votes used in earlier country work. All 72 names were uniquely resolved against the official parliamentary seat register for that date, including the several people surnamed Van Dijk. The public register's `Overlijdensdatum` was checked and empty for all 72. Current public seat records supplied historical/current-role status without assuming that former MPs are deceased.

The [motion](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-25087-331.html) asks for an EU coalition and a national exit-tax proposal for high-net-worth emigrants. Its standalone text does not specify the exact base or a rate. In the [4 April debate](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/kamerstukken/plenaire_verslagen/detail/2023-2024/61), Dijk explicitly describes taxing wealth accumulated before emigration. The legislative researcher independently read this context and recommended inclusion as `exit_tax`, with a development/feasibility qualification. The coordinator separately reopened the original and confirmed that wealth-stock explanation. These records do **not** establish endorsement of an annual domestic wealth tax or an enacted exit levy.

Each evidence row carries that scope qualification. Of the 113 new actors, 62 currently have **only exit-tax evidence**; two other new roll-call actors also gained separate personal statements. This distinction must accompany any account of the increase. Do not report the total as 161 advocates of the same recurring tax design.

The short quotes are contiguous roster extracts containing each member and neighbouring names. Exact roster matching passed for all 72. Identity matching used the member's own recorded name and historical party, not a party-to-member endorsement inference. Spot checks included Paternotte, Pijpelink, Tuinman, Van Baarle, Inge van Dijk, Olger van Dijk, Idsinga, Kostić, Maatoug and Palmen. Tuinman's later correction paragraph explicitly refers to a different foreign-affairs motion; it does not retract the recorded vote on 25087-331.
