## Tax scope and date

The letter expressly asks for an international agreement on wealth taxes and calls for higher taxation of extreme wealth in new national and international tax regimes. Its discussion concerns accumulated private wealth of the richest individuals. It does not confine the request to inheritance, realised capital gains, income or corporate tax. The reference to the earlier corporate minimum-tax agreement is an analogy, followed by an explicit shift to individual wealth. It supplies no annual rate, threshold or levy frequency; wealth_tax_unspecified is appropriate. Do not promote this to annual_net_wealth or billionaire_minimum without a different source.

The organiser release proves publication of this letter on 2023-09-05. The currently accessible roster does not provide individual signing timestamps or independently establish when each name was added. A date of 2023-09-05 is acceptable only as the explicitly disclosed letter-publication date, with the individual signature timing marked unknown. Do not claim that each person signed precisely on that date. A historical roster snapshot would strengthen timing if later needed, but absence of individual timestamps is already disclosed in the proposed records.

## Additional personal publications: Herreveld and Bosman

Checked independently on 2026-10-24 by opening the originals in full, plus Bosman's own biography. No canonical edits.

### Frank Herreveld — PASS

Original: https://www.nlfiscaal.nl/nlfiscaal-doc/4D38622215862CD8C12587FA00329809

The original NLFiscaal opinion identifies Frank Herreveld / F.R. Herreveld as author, carries publication date 7 March 2022, and is numbered NLF Opinie 2022/6. The proposed exact quotation, “een tarief van 1,2% dat geldt boven de belastingvrije voet.”, appears verbatim in the paragraph beginning at extraction line 130.

The next paragraph recommends restoring the old Box 3 levy as an explicitly named wealth tax temporarily while moving towards an accrual tax. He distinguishes this from the contemporaneous progressive 5%-over-EUR-5-million proposal, which he criticises. He nevertheless clearly advocates his own 1.2% stock levy. This is not merely a historical description or advocacy of taxing realised returns.

A / op_ed / partial_wealth is supported. Preserve its limited former Box 3 base and temporary bridge purpose; do not describe him as endorsing the other parties' progressive design or a tax on all net wealth. The source does not propose a one-time levy: temporary legislation can still impose a recurring levy.

### Gerard Bosman — PASS with wording caveat

Original essay: https://gerardbosman.com/2025/05/15/alles-moet-anders-en-wel-nu-werk-en-inkomen-beknopte-versie/

Public-role biography: https://gerardbosman.com/gerard/

The original essay is dated 15 May 2025. Under Vermogensbelastingen, extraction line 543, the proposed quotation appears verbatim: “een aparte vermogensbelasting in voor grote vermogens (boven 1 miljoen euro)”. The same paragraph specifies illustrative progressive rates from 1% up to 5% for billionaires, and a higher rate on exporting wealth abroad. This is a separate stock-of-wealth proposal, distinct from his following corporate profits, dividend and inheritance proposals. Grade A is justified by the threshold and illustrative design; publication is an appropriate evidence type.

The essay presents an ongoing fiscal programme and no one-off wealth levy, so annual_net_wealth is a reasonable recurring-wealth-tax classification. However, the paragraph does not literally specify an annual interval or enumerate every asset / debt-deduction rule. Do not imply those technical details were supplied. Preserve the rates as illustrative, not a final adopted schedule. The wealth-export provision should remain a short design detail; do not conflate it with the separate corporate exit-tax provision later in the essay.

The personal biography establishes a public social-democratic activist, former party/provincial officeholder and PvdA Linksom affiliation. campaigner is a defensible primary actor type. The essay is a personal programme proposal on his own site, not proof of adoption by PvdA, GroenLinks-PvdA or any government. Party policy_adoption or manifesto attribution would overstate the source.
