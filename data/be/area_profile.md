## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Belgium does not currently have a general annual net wealth tax, though it is one of the most hotly contested issues in Belgian politics.
- Securities Account Tax (*Taxe sur les comptes-titres* / *Effectentaks*):
  - In 2018, the Michel I government enacted a 0.15% tax on securities accounts exceeding €500,000, struck down by the Constitutional Court in October 2019 for unequal treatment.
  - In 2021, the De Croo (Vivaldi) government introduced an annual solidarity tax of 0.15% on securities accounts holding €1 million or more (Law of 17 February 2021). Classified under `partial_wealth`.
- Live & Historical Federal Legislative Bills on Net Wealth Tax (54th, 55th, and 56th Legislature):
  - **Chamber DOC 54 0770/001** (*Proposition de loi visant à introduire un impôt sur les grands patrimoines*, 10 Dec 2014):
    - Proposed progressive net wealth tax on estates above €1,250,000.
    - Lead authors / cosponsors: Ahmed Laaouej, Laurette Onkelinx, Stéphane Crusnière, Alain Mathot.
  - **Chamber DOC 55 0036/001** (*Proposition de loi visant à introduire un impôt sur les grands patrimoines*, 9 July 2019):
    - Proposed progressive net wealth tax on estates above €1,250,000.
    - Lead authors / cosponsors: Ahmed Laaouej, Hugues Bayet, Marc Goblet, Frédéric Daerden, Patrick Prévot, Christophe Lacroix, Malik Ben Achour, Sophie Thémont.
  - **Chamber DOC 55 0397/001** (*Proposition de loi instaurant une taxe des millionnaires* / *Wetsvoorstel houdende invoering van een miljonairstaks*, 18 Sept 2019):
    - Proposed rates: 1% (€1m–€2m), 2% (€2m–€3m), 3% (>€3m).
    - Lead authors / cosponsors: Marco Van Hees, Raoul Hedebouw, Peter Mertens, Steven De Vuyst.
  - **Chamber DOC 56 0323/001** (*Proposition de loi instaurant une taxe sur les grands patrimoines*, 8 Oct 2024):
    - Proposed rates: 2% on net wealth between €5m and €10m, 3% on net wealth over €10m (under €5m exempt).
    - Cosponsors: Sofie Merckx, Kemal Bilmez, Peter Mertens, Roberto D’Amico, Raoul Hedebouw, Nadia Moscufo.
  - **Chamber DOC 56 0853/008 Amendment 10** (*Budget des Voies et Moyens 2025*, 18 June 2025):
    - Annual net wealth tax of 2% on wealth >€5m and 3% on wealth >€10m to yield €8 billion.
    - Signatories: Sofie Merckx, Julien Ribaudo, Kemal Bilmez.
  - **Chamber DOC 56 0901/001** (*Proposition de loi visant à introduire un impôt sur les grands patrimoines*, 15 May 2025):
    - Proposed rates: 1% on net wealth between €5m and €100m, 2% on net wealth over €100m.
    - Cosponsors: Paul Magnette, Sophie Thémont, Frédéric Daerden, Caroline Désir, Hugues Bayet, Ludivine Dedonder, Khalil Aouasti, Lydia Mutyebele Ngoi, Christophe Lacroix, Pierre-Yves Dermagne.
  - **Chamber DOC 56 1298/001** (*Wetsvoorstel met het oog op het invoeren van een vermogensbelasting*, 15 Jan 2026):
    - Annual wealth tax on net fortunes exceeding €2.5 million.
    - Cosponsors: Dieter Vanbesien, Sarah Schlitz, Staf Aerts, Meyrem Almaci, Rajae Maouane, Matti Vandemaele, Stefaan Van Hecke, Jeroen Van Lysebettens, Claire Hugon.
- Party Platforms & Proponents:
  - **PTB-PVDA:** Flagship campaign since 2009 for the *miljonairstaks*, championed by federal, regional (Flemish, Walloon, Brussels), and municipal representatives across all major cities (Antwerp, Ghent, Charleroi, Liège, Brussels, Aalst, Sint-Niklaas, Leuven, Seraing, La Louvière).
  - **PS (Parti Socialiste):** Co-initiator (led by Paul Magnette) of the European Citizens' Initiative (ECI) "Tax the Rich" (registered under ECI(2023)000006) and legislative proponent of the 1%–2% wealth tax on >€5m and >€1.25m (DOC 55 0036/001, DOC 54 0770/001).
  - **Groen & Ecolo:** Official 2024 manifesto proposition for a progressive net wealth tax (0.5% to 2% above €1m) and co-sponsors of Chamber DOC 56 1298/001 and Walloon parliamentary interpellations.
  - **Vooruit:** Campaign for a wealth contribution and wealth cadastre, championed by Conner Rousseau and parliamentary group leader Joris Vandenbroucke.
  - **Trade Unions:**
    - **ACV / CSC:** Belgium's largest union confederation, official 2024 brochure (page 23) proposing 1% (€1m–€1.5m), 1.25% (€1.5m–€2m), and 1.5% (>€2m), led by Ann Vermorgen and Marie-Hélène Ska.
    - **ABVV / FGTB:** Major socialist union confederation proposing 0.5% (€1m–€10m) up to 2% (>€10m), led by Thierry Bodson and Miranda Ulens, with sectoral leadership from MWB (Hillal Sor), ACOD Spoor (Tony Fonteyne), and regional federations.
  - **Civil Society & Coalitions:** Financieel Actienetwerk (FAN), Réseau pour la Justice Fiscale (RJF), Financité, Tout Autre Chose, Coalition Climat (Nicolas Van Nuffel), Oxfam Belgique, Hart boven Hard.
  - **High-Net-Worth & Academics:** Bruno Fierens (Millionaires for Humanity), Prof. Paul De Grauwe (LSE / KU Leuven), Dr. Ellen Roelandts (KU Leuven sociologist).
- Opponents (Exclusions):
  - MR (Georges-Louis Bouchez, Benoît Piedboeuf), N-VA (Bart De Wever, Peter De Roover, Ben Weyts), Open Vld (Egbert Lachaert), Les Engagés, DéFI, and CD&V (opposes standalone net wealth tax, favouring only capital gains / dual income tax).
- Polls: Dedicated / Ipsos surveys for *Le Vif* and CNCD-11.11.11 regularly find 70% to 80% of Belgians support a wealth tax on net fortunes exceeding €1 million (excluding the primary residence).
